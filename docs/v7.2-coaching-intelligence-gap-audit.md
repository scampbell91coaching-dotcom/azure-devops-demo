# Traditional Strength V7.2 coaching intelligence gap audit

**Audit date:** 9 August 2026  
**Scope:** repository-declared V7.1 coach and athlete workflows, compared with the four known spreadsheet-workflow gaps in the mission.  
**Decision:** V7.1 is a credible programme authoring and delivery foundation, but it does not yet replace the spreadsheet as the coach's longitudinal decision workspace. A real-athlete beta should not begin until warm-ups, programme timing, actionable review context, and a safe recommendation-to-edit loop are closed.

## Evidence rules and limits

Labels used throughout:

- **Confirmed behaviour** — directly evidenced by current models, services, routes, templates, or tests.
- **Known missing behaviour** — stated in the mission or explicitly acknowledged by current code/UI.
- **Coach validation required** — a likely spreadsheet capability or coaching rule that cannot be established from this checkout.

No `.xlsx`, `.xls`, `.ods`, or coaching `.csv` file exists in the repository. “Spreadsheet parity” is therefore a hypothesis-driven comparison, not a cell-by-cell audit. Before freezing the backlog, the coach should supply one representative recent block, its warm-up tab, weekly review/check-in tab, and any exercise-selection lookup tables, with athlete data anonymised.

This was a read-only product audit. No migration or feature implementation was added.

## Executive findings

1. **Confirmed behaviour:** the platform supports athlete creation, applications, check-ins, programme draft/duplicate/edit/publish/archive, typed prescriptions, lift slots, athlete set logging, and coach review. It also has a 342-exercise catalogue, explainable swap candidates, proposal acceptance/dismissal, provenance, and override records.
2. **Known missing behaviour:** Block Factory adds only accessories manually pinned before preview. It deliberately fills no quota and distributes selected accessories once across days (`portal/block_factory.py:519-595`). This preserves authority but saves little selection work.
3. **Known missing behaviour:** structured training-session warm-ups are absent. `warmup_suitable` metadata exists in the exercise library, while the only complete warm-up planner is meet-day-specific. A session prescription has no warm-up grouping, ramp rule, preparation sequence, or completion model.
4. **Confirmed behaviour, weakly operationalised:** athlete state can store facts, technical observations, constraints, signals, recommendations, and overrides (`portal/models/athlete_state.py`). The live calculator produces only training age, days to competition, latest adherence/fatigue/recovery, session completion, set completion, and RPE adherence (`portal/services/athlete_state.py`). Technical observations and constraints are displayed as prose for review; they do not generate technique-, pressure-, or constraint-specific options.
5. **Confirmed behaviour:** coach authority is structurally protected. A proposal must be previewed; it can be accepted or dismissed; editing a generated proposal requires a reason; source changes invalidate acceptance; overrides are explicit (`portal/block_factory.py:754-968`).
6. **Known missing behaviour:** the closed coaching loop is broken after logging. The dashboard flags only pain and high fatigue, has no programme dates/current-week marker, and cannot calculate “ending soon” (`portal/services/coach_dashboard.py:110-149`). Completed training data is not turned into a review summary or a proposed next-week adjustment.
7. **Product risk:** V7.1 has an auditable intelligence framework ahead of useful coaching intelligence. Generic JSON facts/recommendations, versioned snapshots, HMAC proposal integrity, and override ledgers are sound future foundations, but current recommendations have empty `signal_ids_json` and mostly reproduce coach-entered structure. Beta value should come before further framework expansion.

## Manual work that remains

### Repeated data entry

- Re-key application/intake details into athlete profile/state and then re-enter Factory inputs.
- Enter accessories one by one before Factory can populate them; then manually correct their day placement.
- Create structured warm-ups in free text or outside the platform.
- Enter or calculate training loads because percentage has no confirmed athlete-max resolution path.
- Manually determine the current week and programme end date.
- Navigate between check-ins, athlete dashboard, programme pages and individual logs to perform one weekly review.
- Manually compare prescribed and actual work and decide whether load, reps, RPE, exercise, frequency or volume should change.
- Copy/duplicate weeks, then edit many rows without a block-wide progression grid or batch operation.
- Transfer competition context between programme and Meet Day.

### Judgment work that should remain coach-owned

- Diagnosis and response to pain or injury.
- Interpretation of technique and whether an observed change is causal, transient, or acceptable.
- Exercise selection, progression strategy, deload/return-to-training decisions, competition attempt selection, and final publication.
- Any recommendation where source data is missing, contradictory, stale, or low confidence.

The product should reduce retrieval, comparison, calculation and candidate-generation work. It should not silently make the judgment above.

## Information likely in spreadsheets but absent or weakly represented

Everything in this section is **coach validation required** until representative sheets are reviewed.

### Athlete and environment

- Current training max/e1RM per competition lift, effective date, confidence and source set.
- Preferred stance/grip/bar placement, equipment access, rack/bar/plate constraints, session time cap, training-day availability, schedule exceptions and travel.
- Technique issue taxonomy: phase of lift, direction/change, severity, consistency, cue attempted, response and video reference.
- Pressure/bracing loss: where it occurs, under what load/RPE/reps, whether it changes across sets, and coach interpretation.
- Irritation/pain location, severity, provoking pattern, tolerable alternatives, clinician restrictions and review date—kept explicitly non-diagnostic.

### Programme design

- Calendar dates, current week, phase/mesocycle, deload/taper/meet week and planned competition linkage.
- Per-lift objective, variation rationale, exposure role (technique/volume/intensity), priority and progression rule.
- Accessory slot intent, target tissue/pattern, superset/circuit grouping, substitution hierarchy and equipment fallback.
- General warm-up sequence, movement preparation, lift-specific warm-up/ramp sets, loading/increment rules and time budget.
- Training-max/percentage formulas, rounding, projected loading, set-volume/tonnage, hard-set counts and expected session duration.

### Feedback and decisions

- Session readiness, duration, session RPE, deviation/skip reason, video links, technical grade, bar-speed notes and coach feedback status.
- Weekly trend table combining plan versus actual, readiness, bodyweight, notes, decisions and the reason for each change.
- Explicit next-week decision: keep, progress, regress, swap, cap, deload or investigate, with coach approval and communication state.
- Version/change log visible to athlete: what changed, why, by whom and whether it was seen.

## Gap priorities

### Must have before real-athlete beta

| ID | Gap | Why it gates beta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BETA-01 | Programme calendar/current-week state | Coach and athlete cannot reliably know what is due now; dashboard admits it cannot calculate ending soon. |
| BETA-02 | Structured session warm-ups | A known spreadsheet workflow disappears from the delivered programme; notes are not an adequate safe/usable substitute. |
| BETA-03 | Unified weekly review and action queue | Logged work is not queued or synthesised, so the coach must reconstruct decisions manually and may miss athlete submissions. |
| BETA-04 | Actionable, coach-controlled adjustment proposals | State context currently describes rather than assists. Beta needs small, explainable proposed edits with accept/dismiss/edit, not autonomous programming. |
| BETA-05 | Intake/state confirmation | Programming-relevant application data must be reviewable and confirmed once, with source/freshness, rather than re-keyed or silently inferred. |
| BETA-06 | Safe change/revision visibility | Active-programme edits need an audit/change summary and athlete visibility so the live prescription is trustworthy. |

### Should have during beta

- Assistance candidate shortlists and explicit accessory slots/day placement.
- Programme-wide progression grid and bulk copy/edit with validation.
- Per-lift trend summaries, e1RM estimates labeled as estimates, planned-versus-actual comparisons and data-quality indicators.
- Video/reference links and structured coach observations attached to lift/set/session.
- Session readiness and structured deviation/skip reasons.
- Link blocks to meets and flow openers/results through with coach confirmation.
- Coach-correctable completed logs with immutable revision history.
- Notification/acknowledgement for programme publication and material changes.

### Before external coach SaaS

- Coach/tenant-scoped ownership and authorization for every athlete and artefact; tenant provisioning and deletion/export.
- Configurable taxonomies, templates, units, rounding rules, federations, exercise libraries and recommendation policies per coach.
- Fine-grained audit trail, role delegation, safe support access and retention/privacy controls for video, health-adjacent constraints and imported data.
- Pagination/query scaling, asynchronous notification/import work, monitoring and support tooling based on measured beta usage.
- Versioned template sharing/import/export and an explicit spreadsheet import pathway with validation and rollback.
- Billing/entitlements only after the multi-coach product boundary is validated.

### Later intelligence and automation

- Technique-shift candidates from structured observations plus coach-tagged video—not automated video diagnosis initially.
- Pressure-loss/constraint reasoning that correlates coach labels with load, reps, RPE and phase, shows counter-evidence, and asks for confirmation.
- Individual response models for variation tolerance, volume, frequency, fatigue and recovery.
- Outcome learning from accepted/dismissed/edited recommendations.
- Automated anomaly detection and longer-horizon block suggestions, always as reversible proposals with provenance and confidence.

## Over-engineered relative to present coaching use

These are not necessarily bad designs; they are areas to freeze until beta evidence justifies more work.

1. **Generic athlete-state ledger before capture/use workflows.** Six state tables support versioned facts, signals, recommendations and overrides, but there is little user-facing creation/maintenance and only a small signal set. Do not add more generic state types until BETA-03/04 prove the workflow.
2. **Proposal integrity and override machinery around a low-value proposal.** HMAC replay protection, stale-source comparison, recommendation statuses and replacement ledgers are robust, but the proposed block mostly reflects form inputs and static templates. Keep the safety boundary; invest next in useful proposal content.
3. **Duplicated template systems.** Database `DayTemplate`/`DayTemplateExercise`, hard-coded `programming_templates.py`, intelligence JSON templates, and Block Factory fallbacks create several sources of truth. Consolidate only after coach-approved templates are known; do not add another abstraction.
4. **Broad prescription schema ahead of common workflows.** Six modes plus many nullable targets cover edge cases, while basic block-wide editing, calendar scheduling and training-max resolution are missing. Validate which modes the coach actually uses; hide or defer low-use modes rather than expanding them.
5. **High-resolution-looking exercise metadata with low-resolution evidence.** Fatigue is effectively bucketed by broad category, and some swap groups/equipment options are intentionally ambiguous. Do not pretend this supports precise fatigue optimisation or constraint safety.
6. **Separate `split` and frequency inputs.** The generated day sequence is driven by requested frequency/training days; `split` mainly survives in the request/proposal. Either make split a real constraint/template choice or remove it from the primary workflow after coach validation.
7. **Platform operations surfaces are mature relative to product loop.** This repository has extensive infrastructure, security, observability and release-readiness work. That is valuable, but product investment should now prioritize the coaching loop before deeper SaaS/platform decomposition.

## V7.2 backlog with acceptance criteria

### V7.2-01 — Programme schedule and current week (`BETA-01`)

**Outcome:** every active programme has an unambiguous calendar position.

Acceptance criteria:

- Coach can set a block start date and map ordered weeks/sessions to dates without changing their authored order.
- Coach and athlete views identify current, past and upcoming weeks consistently in a specified timezone.
- Dashboard “ending soon” is computed from stored dates and tested at boundary dates; it never guesses when dates are absent.
- Rescheduling records who changed what and preserves completed log associations.
- Existing undated blocks render with an explicit “schedule not set” state.

### V7.2-02 — Structured training warm-up plans (`BETA-02`)

**Outcome:** spreadsheet warm-ups are represented without confusing them with working sets.

Acceptance criteria:

- Coach can attach an ordered reusable warm-up plan to a session, with purpose/category, exercise, dosage/time and coach notes.
- Coach can define lift-specific ramp sets as explicit weights or a reviewed rule based on the work-set target; rounding and bar weight are visible.
- Athlete view clearly separates general preparation, lift warm-ups and working sets.
- Warm-up completion is optional and does not contaminate working-set completion, volume or RPE-adherence signals.
- Catalogue filtering uses `warmup_suitable`, but the coach may explicitly author/override with rationale.
- At least one anonymised historical spreadsheet warm-up can be represented without material loss, confirmed by the coach.

### V7.2-03 — Weekly coach review workspace (`BETA-03`)

**Outcome:** one page supports the review-to-decision workflow.

Acceptance criteria:

- Queue includes newly completed sessions as well as check-ins, with unread/reviewed state and oldest/riskiest visibility.
- Athlete-week view combines prescribed versus actual sets, completion/skips, notes, check-in, bodyweight/recovery context and prior coach response.
- Each signal shows window, freshness, sample count and source links; missing data remains explicit.
- Sleep, stress, motivation, recovery and adherence are not silently dropped from risk context.
- Coach can record a weekly decision and response without navigating to unrelated dashboards.

### V7.2-04 — Explainable adjustment proposals (`BETA-04`)

**Outcome:** the platform suggests bounded edits while the coach remains the decision-maker.

Acceptance criteria:

- First release supports a small coach-approved rule set such as hold, reduce/increase one load or RPE target, reduce a set, or present swap candidates; it does not diagnose or auto-publish.
- Every proposal identifies target athlete/week/session/prescription, exact source records, rule/version, rationale, uncertainty/missing data and before/after values.
- Coach can accept, edit with reason, dismiss with optional reason, or take no action; only acceptance writes a draft change.
- Coach reviews a complete diff before publication; accepted changes are reversible and auditable.
- Contradictory/stale evidence suppresses the proposal or displays the conflict.
- Tests prove recommendation generation never changes an active programme by itself.

### V7.2-05 — Intake-to-state confirmation (`BETA-05`)

**Outcome:** programming inputs are captured once with provenance.

Acceptance criteria:

- From an application/athlete page, coach sees candidate values mapped to defined athlete/programming fields.
- Each field is explicitly accept/edit/skip; no application text is silently transformed into a fact.
- Confirmed values retain source, effective date and actor; conflicts with existing values are shown.
- Factory pre-fills confirmed days/frequencies/competition context but keeps all values editable.
- Required-for-programming missing fields are visible before preview.

### V7.2-06 — Programme revision and athlete communication (`BETA-06`)

**Outcome:** athlete and coach can trust what changed in a live programme.

Acceptance criteria:

- Material active-programme edits create an append-only revision summary with actor, time, reason and before/after values.
- Coach previews changes before they become athlete-visible.
- Athlete sees a clear “updated” notice and change summary; acknowledgement state is recorded if enabled.
- Historical session logs retain prescription snapshots and are never rewritten by later programme edits.

### V7.2-07 — Assistance slots and candidate shortlist

**Outcome:** Factory reduces accessory search without taking selection authority.

Acceptance criteria:

- Coach defines or selects day-specific assistance slots with intent (pattern/tissue/technical purpose), priority, count range and equipment constraints.
- Preview offers at most a coach-approved bounded number of candidates per slot, ranked with plain-language reasons from catalogue fields and athlete constraints.
- Nothing is inserted until the coach selects it; zero assistance remains valid.
- Coach controls day placement/order and can pin a choice across weeks.
- Ambiguous swap groups are excluded until domain-reviewed; tests cover constraint/equipment filtering and missing metadata.

### V7.2-08 — Block-wide progression editor

**Outcome:** common spreadsheet editing speed is restored.

Acceptance criteria:

- Coach can compare all weeks for an exercise/lift slot in one view and edit sets/reps/load/RPE without opening every session.
- Copy-across and bounded progression actions show a full diff and validate prescription rules before save.
- Main-lift exposure counts, duplicate positions and invalid prescription combinations are visibly blocked.
- Unsaved and concurrent edits fail safely; no partial block update occurs.

## V7.3 backlog with acceptance criteria

### V7.3-01 — Performance and response trends

- Show per-lift/exercise planned versus actual load/reps/RPE, completion, best-set and estimated-strength trends over coach-selectable windows.
- Any e1RM/model names its formula, input set and uncertainty; coach can exclude invalid sets.
- No trend is shown below a coach-approved minimum sample; missingness and programme changes are visible.

### V7.3-02 — Structured technical observation and evidence

- Coach can tag lift, phase, observed issue, severity/consistency, cue/intervention, outcome and optional set/video reference.
- Free text remains available; the platform never labels an issue from video automatically.
- Active versus resolved/superseded observations are clear and appear beside relevant programming decisions.

### V7.3-03 — Constraint-aware substitutions

- Candidate service considers reviewed exercise setup tags, available equipment and active non-diagnostic constraints.
- Every inclusion/exclusion has an explanation; absence of metadata is not treated as safety.
- Coach chooses the substitution and scope (set/session/week/block), previews the diff and can revert.

### V7.3-04 — Programme/Meet Day integration

- A block can link to one meet and derive days-to-meet from that record rather than duplicated text.
- Coach can explicitly promote planned openers to Meet Day and confirm meet results back into athlete history.
- No attempt or training max is silently changed.

### V7.3-05 — Recommendation outcome feedback

- Accepted, edited and dismissed proposals retain source signals and final applied diff.
- Coach can record whether the change helped, was neutral or was unsuitable with an optional reason.
- Reporting distinguishes recommendation acceptance from athlete outcome; it does not claim causality.

### V7.3-06 — Spreadsheet import/parity tool

- Import supports a coach-approved versioned template, preview, row-level validation and all-or-nothing commit.
- Unknown exercises/fields and formula cells are reported; nothing is guessed.
- Imported records retain workbook/sheet/row provenance without retaining unnecessary personal data.
- A representative historical block, warm-up plan and review history round-trip with coach-confirmed material parity.

### V7.3-07 — External-coach readiness discovery

- Conduct workflow research with at least three external coaches and document shared versus coach-specific concepts.
- Define tenancy, authorization, export/deletion, audit, template ownership and configuration boundaries before implementation.
- Reassess which V7.1 abstractions should be simplified based on measured beta use.

## Recommended delivery order

1. Obtain and anonymise representative spreadsheets; run a coach parity workshop and lock vocabulary.
2. Deliver schedule/current week and structured warm-ups.
3. Deliver the unified weekly review surface and training-log review queue.
4. Add the smallest useful adjustment proposals with explicit diffs and authority controls.
5. Add intake confirmation and live-programme revision visibility.
6. Trial assistance slots/candidates and the block-wide editor during beta.
7. Use beta evidence to select V7.3 trend, technical-observation and integration work.

## Coach validation checklist

- Which exact spreadsheet tabs are still opened for every athlete and at what point in the week?
- What warm-up structure must be preserved: general sequence, lift ramp, both, or athlete-specific variants?
- How are accessories chosen and placed: tissue, movement, technical issue, time, equipment, preference, pain response, or progression history?
- What does “pressure loss” mean operationally for squat, bench and deadlift, and what evidence makes the coach act?
- Which technique shifts deserve a programme change versus a cue or observation only?
- What are the coach's actual weekly adjustment rules and hard “never suggest” boundaries?
- Which prescription modes and spreadsheet formulas are used in the last ten programmes?
- When may a live programme be edited, and what must the athlete be told?
- Which spreadsheet outputs would make the coach refuse beta if absent?

## Release recommendation

**Do not call V7.2 spreadsheet replacement complete from repository evidence alone.** Start a controlled real-athlete beta only after V7.2-01 through V7.2-06 pass with anonymised historical parity examples and coach sign-off. Keep recommendations advisory, source-linked, reversible and unpublished until explicitly accepted. Freeze additional generic intelligence infrastructure until the weekly review and adjustment loop proves it saves coaching time without obscuring judgment.
