# V7.8 athlete service experience

## Decision

The existing `AthleteCheckinSettings` flags are the V7.8 source of truth for athlete-facing service access. `training_enabled` controls training surfaces, `nutrition_enabled` controls nutrition surfaces, and the weekly check-in appears only when its workflow is active and at least one service module is enabled. Existing athletes without settings retain the established training-only default.

This is deliberately schema-independent. It avoids a migration while giving future work one adapter (`athlete_services`) to replace when a dedicated entitlement source exists.

## Composition by scenario

| Scenario | Primary navigation | Dashboard order and primary action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Training only | Today, Programme, Check-in, Account | Training first; “View session” is primary. Check-in, training trends, coach response and account follow. Nutrition links, cards and empty states are absent. |
| Nutrition only | Today, Check-in, Nutrition, Account | Check-in is first when active, followed by nutrition. “Complete check-in” is primary when due; otherwise “Add nutrition check-in” is primary. Programme links, training cards and training-only trend content are absent. |
| Training + nutrition | Today, Programme, Check-in, Nutrition, Account | Training remains first and “View session” remains the calm primary action. Nutrition is additive and does not displace the next session. |
| No active modules | Today, Account | Coach responses and account context remain available. No service CTA is invented. |

Desktop and mobile navigation use the same service rules. A hidden service is also rejected at its athlete programme/check-in/nutrition entry route, so copied bookmarks cannot lead to irrelevant screens.

## Empty-state policy

An enabled service may show a useful readiness state, such as a programme being prepared or the first nutrition submission not yet existing. A disabled service must render nothing: no card, anchor link, navigation item, trend placeholder or CTA. This distinguishes “included but not ready” from “not part of this athlete’s coaching”.

## Future meet-day and video entitlements

Future add-ons should extend the service-access adapter with explicit flags; they should not infer entitlement from the presence of historical records.

- Do not reserve permanent bottom-navigation slots today.
- When enabled and actionable, show one compact dashboard item below the core service cards (for example, “Meet plan ready” or “New video feedback”).
- Promote an add-on into primary navigation only if it becomes a repeated athlete workflow, not merely because the entitlement exists.
- Keep dormant or not-yet-configured add-ons out of the athlete UI. Coaches can handle setup state in their workspace.
- Prefer an “More” destination if three or more add-ons become routinely navigable; do not expand the mobile bar beyond five items.

This keeps today’s interface quiet while allowing entitlements to add relevant actions later.

## Verification

Python route/template tests cover training-only and nutrition-only composition, including rejection of a disabled programme. Playwright scenarios cover training-only and training-plus-nutrition navigation and dashboard composition, including direct rejection of the disabled nutrition entry route.
